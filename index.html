THE URBANTHREAD SPECIFICATION

WHY WE BUILD IN 240 GSM

Fast fashion t-shirts warp, shrink, and fade in 3 washes. We mill high-twist compact combed cotton that maintains its structural drape year after year.

01

Heavyweight Structure

240 GSM French terry and jersey weight gives a distinct architectural drape that never clings or wrinkles awkwardly.

02

Bio-Washed & Pre-Shrunk

Subjected to natural enzyme wash to eliminate surface lint and pre-shrunk so your size stays true from day 1 to day 500.

03

Reinforced Lycra Rib Collar

1x1 heavy ribbed collar with 5% elastane prevents neck stretching and bacon-collars, staying crisp wear after wear.

04

Pan-India Express Dispatch

Every piece is dispatched from our Indiranagar Bengaluru warehouse within 24 hours. Delivered across India in 3–7 business days.
