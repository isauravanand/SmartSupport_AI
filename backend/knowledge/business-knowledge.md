## 3. Sizing & Fit Guidance

- **Standard Fit Guide:**
  - **S (Small):** 38-inch chest, 30-inch waist.
  - **M (Medium):** 40-inch chest, 32-inch waist.
  - **L (Large):** 42-inch chest, 34-inch waist.
  - **XL (Extra Large):** 44-inch chest, 36-inch waist.
  - **XXL (Double Extra Large):** 46-inch chest, 38-inch waist.
- **Oversized Collection Note:** Items labelled "Oversized" are pre-designed with a boxy, drop-shoulder silhouette. Customers seeking a relaxed streetwear look should choose their regular size. Customers seeking a snug regular fit should order one size smaller.
- **Fabric Care Instructions:** Machine wash cold (inside-out) on gentle cycle with like colors. Do not bleach. Tumble dry low or hang dry in shade. Warm iron inside-out; do not iron directly on graphics or prints.

## 4. Shipping & Delivery Policy

- **Order Processing:** Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on business days are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours.
- **Delivery Timeline:** Standard courier delivery takes **3 to 7 business days** depending on the destination pin code (major metros: 3–4 days; tier 2/3 cities: 5–7 days).
- **Shipping Rates:**
  - **Free Shipping:** Applicable on all orders above ₹999.
  - **Standard Shipping:** Flat ₹79 shipping fee for orders under ₹999.
- **Order Tracking:** Once dispatched, customers receive an automated SMS and email containing a live tracking link and courier AWB number. Customers can also track shipments by asking the chatbot or visiting the order tracking portal.

## 5. Returns & Exchange Policy

- **Return Window:** Returns are accepted **within 7 days of delivery**.
- **Eligible Conditions for Return:**
  - Products must be completely unwashed, unworn, undamaged, and free from perfume or deodorant marks.
  - Original price tags, brand labels, and protective packaging must remain intact.
- **Exchange Policy:**
  - Size and color exchanges are free of charge, **subject to stock availability** in our fulfillment centers.
  - If the requested exchange size is out of stock, customers may choose an alternate color, store credit, or a complete refund.
- **Non-Returnable Items:**
  - For hygiene reasons, socks, innerwear, face masks, and items purchased during clearance "Final Sale" promotions cannot be returned or exchanged.
- **Reverse Pickup:** We arrange free reverse doorstep courier pickup for approved returns across 19,000+ Indian postal codes.
