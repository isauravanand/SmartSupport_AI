3. SIZING AND FIT GUIDANCE
============================================================
Standard Sizing Chart:
- S (Small): 38-inch chest, 30-inch waist
- M (Medium): 40-inch chest, 32-inch waist
- L (Large): 42-inch chest, 34-inch waist
- XL (Extra Large): 44-inch chest, 36-inch waist
- XXL (Double Extra Large): 46-inch chest, 38-inch waist

Fit Guidance:
Oversized collection items are designed with an intentionally relaxed, boxy drop-shoulder silhouette. Choose your normal size for an oversized look, or size down for a standard regular fit.

Fabric Care:
Machine wash cold inside-out with like colors. Do not bleach. Tumble dry low or hang dry in shade. Warm iron inside-out; do not iron directly on printed graphics.
